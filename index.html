The Comfort Plan

The cheapest repair is the one you never need

Quarterly maintenance catches worn parts, dirty coils, and low refrigerant before they turn into a dead system on the hottest day of the year. Comfort Plan members get:

  • Four tune-ups a year — two before cooling season, two before heating season
  • A real tune-up every visit: coils cleaned, condensate drain cleared, refrigerant pressures checked, electrical connections tightened, capacitors and safety controls tested, filter changed, and a carbon monoxide test on the furnace
  • Priority scheduling — members go to the front of the line when something breaks
  • A written condition report after every visit, so you always know where your system stands
  • Transfers with the house — sell the place, and the remaining plan goes to the new owner

One flat price per system, billed yearly or monthly. Repairs are always quoted before any work starts.
